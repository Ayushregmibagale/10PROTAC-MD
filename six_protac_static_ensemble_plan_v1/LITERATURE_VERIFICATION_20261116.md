# Literature verification — 16 November 2026

Scope: primary publisher/repository records and accessible methods were checked; this is not a complete replication, supplement review or code audit of every paper. Some direct publisher/PMC pages were blocked. Earlier conversational summaries are not verified methods specifications.

1. PROTACMap: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13321-026-01268-5 . Full publisher methods accessible. Includes CRBN and VHL benchmarking (34 structures), with new WEE1/PKMYT1 applications focused on CRBN. Earlier blanket claim that the approach is CRBN-only is incorrect. Reported33/34structural recovery is not97%degradation classification. Uses MD as well as static geometry; our static-only adaptation must not inherit its entire validation. Repository https://github.com/Husam-PSE/PROTACMap was opened, not audited.

2. COMPASS: https://chemistry-europe.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/cmdc.70385?af=R . Accessible full publisher text describes20000RosettaDockconformations perprotein pair, clustering, CRLclash exclusion, E2Cys85–LysNZdistance<=60A with3Aflexibility margin, and line-of-sight. Abstract describes a high-sensitivity negative filter,20crystalstructures and112PROTACs across8systems,93%recall; recall is not specificity or overall accuracy. Repository https://github.com/ssueron/compass opened, not audited. Reusing protein-pair sampling across compatible linkers is a scaling hypothesis requiring verification, not automatic6x savings.

3. Kumar/Sobhia: https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jpcb.5c07053 ; primary abstract https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41577441/ . Representative target is PTP1B, NOT BRD4BD2 as earlier claimed. Abstract verifies6900+ternaryposes, fullCRL2VHLmodeling, RMSDclustering, lysine proximity and dynamics. Detailed docking engine, specific50Acutoff and claimed BRD4lysines were NOT established by this abstract and must not be copied into our protocol without fulltext/supplementverification.

4. VHLhomodimerizer preprint: https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.11.18.689129v1 . Direct page blocked. Authors publication listing https://sites.dundee.ac.uk/alessio-ciulli/publications/ and authors-lab journalclub https://sites.dundee.ac.uk/alessio-ciulli/wp-content/uploads/sites/233/2026/02/CeTPD_Dec_Jan.pdf support VHLhomoPROTACsystem and stable nonproductive assemblies. Supports general principle, not direct ABL1/AXL experimental proof or proof of our specific mechanism/novelty.

5. SE(3)-PROTACs: primary abstract https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42108630/ and publisher https://academic.oup.com/bib/article/27/3/bbag228/8675354?searchresult=1 . Encodes3DPROTACsubstructure molecular graphs plus protein-sequenceESMembeddings; not simply an experimentally docked ternary-contactgraph GNN. Abstract reports1979samples,80.81%random-split accuracy,65.62%cluster-split and64.08%temporal-split accuracy. Useful comparator, does not validate our eight-target GNN.

6. Integrative structural/computational analysis: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666386426003267 ; DOI10.1016/j.xcrp.2026.103420 . Publisher summary supports MDframework, lysinetopology, designed/synthesized CDK12degrader withDC50=6.5nM,Dmax98.5%. MD-specific validation cannot be claimed for our static-onlyscreen. Authors repository https://github.com/Alfredo-Elvis/MD-Based-PROTAC-SDR/blob/main/README.md supports source availability; no fullcodeauditperformed.

## Consequences for campaign

Benchmark structural recovery/ranking first. Use linker-specific feasibility, clustering and physically checked CRL/lysine accessibility as separate baselines/features. COMPASS and PROTACMap are relevant methodological comparators, not guaranteed drop-in replacements. Reject unsupported claims that50Aautomatically passes allkinases,20000poses is inherently infeasible, or a staticSASAproxy has proven0.936correlation without checking saved data. Do not equate recovery, recall and degradation prediction accuracy. No change to the user no-MD constraint.
